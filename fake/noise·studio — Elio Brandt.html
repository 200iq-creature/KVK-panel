About

Elio Brandt writes small programs that draw, based in Porto. The work sits between plotting, weather and old textile patterns.

The studio started in 2020 as a notebook of sketches and slowly turned into a practice: flow fields, packing problems, erosion, and the occasional failed attempt at teaching a machine to hesitate.

Every image here is computed in your browser from a single number. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored; close the tab and the piece is gone unless you remember its seed.

Commissions for print and installation are open a few times a year.
